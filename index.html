Clean Launcher

Installation, Features & User Guide

⚠️ Before making any changes, always back up your /Mods folder using the Backup feature in More Tools ➡️ Settings & Utilities. This s protect your game if anything goes wrong.
Getting Started

When you first open Clean Launcher, you’ll be asked whether you want to set up an Auto Mods folder and allow Clean Launcher to start with your computer.

Auto Mods creates a new folder inside \Documents\Electronic Arts\The Sims 4\Mods. Anything you place in this folder (files, folders, or zip archives) will be automatically extracted and sorted. Tray and Save files are moved to their correct locations, while other mod files are extracted and kept neatly organized inside Auto Mods.

If you choose to start Clean Launcher with Windows, Auto Mods will always be active in the background. If you choose not to, you’ll need to open Clean Launcher for the Auto Mods folder to be monitored and sorted.

It’s perfect for download sprees. You can right-click the folder, pin it to Quick Access, and drag your downloads straight into it—no manual extraction required.

You can enable or disable Auto Mods at any time via Settings ➡️ Toggle Auto Mods, or by right-clicking Clean Launcher in the system tray. If the folder is deleted while still enabled, it will be recreated automatically.

You can also drag and drop files into Clean Launcher to have them automatically extracted and sorted, even if Auto Mods is disabled.

Clean & Launch Sims 4

Cleans cache files and error logs, then launches The Sims 4, all in one click.

You can optionally clear only the cache when your \Mods folder has changed, and choose a Mods Profile (see 👤 Manage Mod Profiles) to quickly switch between different CC and gameplay setups.

Don't want your game to update? Click Clean & Open EA App instead.

Scan Mods Folder

Scans your \Mods folder for common issues, including duplicate files, hidden scripts, zipped mods, missing files, and more.

You’ll get a clear summary of everything found, with expandable sections for each issue.

Review & Fix will give you a preview of the issue, and will prompt you through fixing it.

Backup Mods, Saves & Tray

Zip up your Mods, Saves, or Tray to create a backup, or to transfer to a new computer.

Back up everything all at once, or choose between /Mods, /Saves and /Tray. You can choose exactly where your backups are saved.

Organize Mods Folder

View your \Mods folder, drag and drop files to move them, select multiple items, and create or delete folders with ease. Right-click any folder to add an icon, or prevent it from being moved by Sort For Me.

Sort For Me will organize loose mods into folders for you, giving you a preview before you go ahead. You can Undo any unwanted moves.

Manage Mod Profiles

Create different profiles for different playstyles, and switch between them easily. If new CC is detected and you’ll be prompted before switching, and your Default Mods (the folder you started off with) is backed up safely.

For example, you could have a /Mods just for historical gameplay, and then switch back to your Default Mods for everyday play.

Updated a mod? When switching back to a profile, you’ll be prompted to update the mod in that profile too.

Settings

Update your /Mods folder and game path locations, or reset them to their defaults. Manage Launch settings, enable or disable OneDrive warnings, toggle the Auto Mods folder, and choose whether Clean Launcher starts with Windows.

You can also generate a Full Report, which creates a detailed list of your Mods folder to make troubleshooting and support much easier. Help directs you to my website, where you can Contact Me if you need some assistance.

Help & FAQ
  • Report an Issue
    Create Full Report from the Settings menu, and send it to me on Discord, or through my support page.

    If your issue is with a particular mod, I'll need your midnitetech_log.html too. With my midnitetech_modlibrary installed, open the cheat console in-game (Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+C) and enter mtlog. Repeat whatever you were trying to do, exit the game, and send me the log from the location it gives you.

  • Uninstall
    Uninstall from Add/Remove Programs. Ensure you right-click and exit Clean Launcher first, from the Tray menu (by the clock on your computer.) You can remove any AutoStart entries from the Settings menu, or from Start-up Apps in your Task Manager.
  • Languages & Translations

    Clean Launcher is shipped in English, but you can add your own translations that you've made yourself, or that someone else has uploaded. Find the option to import a new language you've downloaded in Settings.

  • Installation

    If you run into any issues downloading Clean Launcher, your browser or anti-virus could be blocking the download. This is because it's a unsigned app, (like Sims4Studio). You can view the VirusTotal scan report here, to prove it's safe to use.

    To solve browser-related issues, click Ctrl+J and ensure it's not being blocked. If Windows SmartScreen pops up, just click More info... and Run Anyway. If your anti-virus blocks it, you can whitelist it. Depending on which anti-virus you have, you can Google how to whitelist an app.

    • Tip: Use the Backup feature regularly to keep your /Mods folder safe. You can also use it to transfer your mods, saves, and tray files to a new computer.
